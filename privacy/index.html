Privacy Policy — CFB Peter Publisher

CFB Peter Publisher is a personal tool that posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own

TikTok account.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's TikTok account, through TikTok Login Kit, with the

permissions to read basic profile info and to upload and publish videos.

  • What it stores: an access token and refresh token, kept in a private file on the owner's

own computer. Nothing is stored on any server.

  • What it shares: nothing. No data is collected from anyone else, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under

Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services.
